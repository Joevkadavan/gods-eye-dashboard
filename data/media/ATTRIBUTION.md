# Sample media attribution

The four demonstration clips in this directory were obtained from the archived `intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos` repository, which identifies its content under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0): https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos and https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos/blob/master/LICENSE

Included files: `car-detection.mp4`, `one-by-one-person-detection.mp4`, `person-bicycle-car-detection.mp4`, and `store-aisle-detection.mp4`.

These clips are illustrative inference samples, not labelled evaluation data. Attribution does not imply they are representative or suitable for training. The face-demographics example clips in the source repository were deliberately not downloaded or used.
